This is a fairly exhaustive list of issued patents which I am a named inventor. A former employer routinely filed continuation-in-part applications — those are grouped by family.


I update this page from time to time. Alternatively, [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/?inventor=Zachary+Shiner&status=GRANT&dups=language) has a recent list.

| ----------- | ----------- |
| Lighting fixture systems<br>[US&nbsp;12320492](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12320492B1/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Leviton Manufacturing Company</span> | A modular linear lighting fixture system designed to improve alignment, serviceability, and optical performance when connecting multiple fixtures. By offsetting the joints of the housing and the lens, incorporating light-blocking sidewalls, and using removable liners with snap-fit electrical components, the system reduces light leakage at fixture junctions and simplifies maintenance or reconfiguration. |
| Drive assembly and motor assembly for strap tensioning<br>[US&nbsp;20250083846A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250083846A1/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: ABB Group</span> | A motorized tool designed to bundle multiple wires or cables, using a tape that is tensioned and sealed around the bundle. The innovation lies in a drive and gear assembly that enables bi-directional tape movement via a compliant member and gear train, allowing for precise control during tape feeding, tightening, and securing operations. |
| Bone stabilization systems<br>[US&nbsp;11871970](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11871970/), [US&nbsp;11096730](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11096730/), [US&nbsp;12279795](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12279795/), [US&nbsp;12318122](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12318122B2), [US&nbsp;12390254](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12390254B2/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Globus Medical</span> | Distal Femur aiming guide system that securely attaches to a bone plate using a threaded post with alignment features, ensuring precise orientation and alignment of surgical screw paths. Its novel connection mechanism—including a boss, ball-end pins, and a slotted arm—makes the aiming guide fast to install and resistant to misalignment during minimally invasive procedures. |
| Cerclage cable system and apparatus [US&nbsp;11813011](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11813011/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Globus Medical</span> | Cerclage cable system that integrates with a bone plate using modular securing devices, allowing surgeons to loop and tension cables around bones without bulky hardware or excessive steps. A specialized cable tensioner with precise locking, adjustment, and release mechanisms makes it easier to apply consistent, controlled tension and streamline the entire fixation process. |
| Bone Stabilization Systems<br>[US&nbsp;10905477](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10905477/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Globus Medical</span> | Proximal Tibial plating system designed to stabilize fractures using a combination of locking and non-locking screw holes offering flexibility in surgical fixation. It also includes aiming guides that help surgeons accurately position screws during procedures, improving efficiency and alignment in trauma care. |
| Syndesmosis fixation assembly<br>[US&nbsp;11723647](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11723647/), [US&nbsp;12114850](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12114850/)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Globus Medical</span> | Syndesmosis fixation device that secures stabilizing sutures without needing to pass completely through the fibula, addressing a key limitation of current screw and button-based systems. Its novel design uses a rotating suture lock integrated with a bone-anchored implant, allowing controlled tensioning and fixation with minimal bone disruption. |
| External fixator assembly<br>[US&nbsp;11672566](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11672566/), [US&nbsp;10722268](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10722268/), [US&nbsp;10682160](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10682160/), [US&nbsp;10070890](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10070890/), [US&nbsp;12376885](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12376885B2)<br><span class="assignee">Assignee: Globus Medical</span> | A modular external fixator system used to stabilize fractured bones with improved speed and flexibility. Its design allows multiple clamp assemblies to rotate freely for alignment during setup, then lock firmly in place—making the system quicker to apply and more adaptable in trauma care situations. |
