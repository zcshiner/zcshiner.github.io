🪞 The Doppelgänger: July 7, 2026

Orange Line car 1521. Red Line car 1521. Same day. Same person. Two entirely different trains built roughly half a century apart, on two different lines, sharing a number because the MBTA's numbering plan treats uniqueness as a stylistic choice.

It happened again in slower motion with car 1517: Red Line on August 1, Orange Line on August 12. Two number collisions, both logged, both real. The roster writes the Red Line ones with a leading zero (01517, 01521) specifically to prevent this, and it did not help.
