🏛️ Four Generations in 36 Rides

🏺 Red Line Archaeology

1517 and 1521 are #1 cars — the last 18 survivors of a 1969–70 Pullman-Standard order, and only 14 of those remain in the 01500s. They are being scrapped as CRRC #4s arrive. You caught two of them with time to spare, and 1517 twice.

Married pairs ridden end to end: 1874/1875 (#3) and 1918/1919 (#4). The #2s are 1740, 1753 and 1756 — the last of those the second-to-last number in the entire 01700–01757 series. 1903 comes from the very first #4 delivery batch and is now the Red Line's only three-time car: the oldest new train, and the only regular.

Sixteen of your 33 Red Line cars are in series slated for replacement. This log is quietly becoming a historical record.

🪞 The Doppelgänger: July 7, 2026

Orange Line car 1521. Red Line car 1521. Same day. Same person. Two entirely different trains built roughly half a century apart, on two different lines, sharing a number because the MBTA's numbering plan treats uniqueness as a stylistic choice.

It happened again in slower motion with car 1517, which has since doubled down: both versions have now been ridden twice. The roster writes the Red Line ones with a leading zero (01517, 01521) specifically to prevent this, and it did not help.

Closest call since: bus 1961 and Red Line car 1960 — whose married partner is, naturally, 1961. One car away from the first bus-and-train collision. Bus 1982 has no Red Line twin yet; the #4 deliveries had only reached 1967 on the latest roster. Give CRRC time.

👑 The Triple Crown — and now the Grand Slam

Three fleets in one day was first achieved on July 7 (CR + OL + RL, with the Doppelgänger thrown in, which was frankly showing off). It has since become routine: ten three-fleet days in all, seven of them the classic Commuter Rail + Orange + Red. The Green Line has joined in twice.

The Grand Slam: four fleets in one day. Long considered out of reach because of the Bus. Then October 14: Commuter Rail, Orange Line, Red Line and a bus, all in a single day. It happened again less than three weeks later. Five fleets remains undone — the Green Line only gets ridden on Saturdays, and the Orange Line never does.

🎄 The Dec 19 Double: #882, twice in one day

Morning train: Car 882. Evening train: also Car 882. The probability of matching on any given two-trip day is roughly 0.23% (1-in-442). Across your 69 two-trip CR days: a ~14% chance this ever happens at all. It happened on December 19th. Cherish this.

Eleven months and 398 rides later, it has not happened again. Not once, in any fleet, in any mode.
